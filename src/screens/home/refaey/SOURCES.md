# Refaey: sources for every claim

For Goda's `/about` page. Every claim below was checked against the page it cites on **2026-11-14** (the day the facts were recorded) or later the same weekend. A claim that no opened page confirmed was cut from the script; those are listed at the end.

Line ids refer to `dialogues/dialogues.ts` (`b…` the briefing, `a…` Apollo, `c…` Chang'e 4, `t…` Athena). `READ-ALOUD.txt` has every line in play order, all branches.

## What is fiction, what is real

**Fiction** (said on screen in b7r, at the end of the touchdown card, and in the /missions subtitle): Refaey, his lander and its callsign (Shams, Ostora-1 or Horus), his slipped launch, the failed valve, and the jokes (Cairo traffic, microbuses in Ramses Square, the warm socks, "Easier is boring"). A joke never carries a number or a fact.

**Real and sourced**: every mission, date, angle and duration below. Cards on the planner screens show once per session whether or not the briefing was played; the Compare dates card was dropped because no one-line card fits that header without moving the comparison cards.

## Claims that were cut

Not confirmed by a page that could be opened:

- *Page does not say it*: Armstrong "could see every rock because each threw a long, sharp shadow"; "early lunar morning" and the Sun "behind him"; shadows "swallowing" the ground; Athena's "panels facing the wrong way" and "deep cold"; "a day later Intuitive Machines said it did not expect Athena to recharge"; a "small" crater; libration amplitudes in degrees ("a few degrees up and down"); "your thumb at arm's length is about 2°" (NASA's own page gives the little finger, about 1°).
- *Could not confirm this pass*: "That's where NASA is heading next" as the reason for the south pole (a5b keeps only the joke: only search snippets, no opened NASA page, and NASA's current Artemis plan has moved); "Armstrong took manual control and flew past the boulders" is no longer in the script (it was confirmed on the ALSJ landing page but the new story does not use it).
- *Could not open*: AMNH "Skylight: Apollo Mission Landings and Lunar Phases" (HTTP 403); Space.com's Queqiao article (only the headline loaded); Intuitive Machines' investor release (HTTP 403); NASA NSSDC Chang'e 4 record (error page); NASA Ames "thumb" page (connection refused).

## Art and AI use (for `/about`)

Refaey, the animated astronaut (idle, walking, hop) and the busts, are 3D renders of an original character: **"Refaey's 3D renders were generated with [TOOL — fill in before 13:00] (original character)."** The frames were cleaned and cut into animation strips with `refaey/art/tools/make_sprites.py` (sources in `refaey/art/source/`; WhatsApp-compressed contact sheets, so the edges were re-feathered and the colour fringe replaced; measurements in `art/tools/last-run.json`). The older flat illustrations (Claude Design) are no longer used anywhere.

His lines were drafted with Claude and then checked by the team against the sources listed above.
