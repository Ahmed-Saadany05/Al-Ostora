## Claims that were cut

Not confirmed by a page that could be opened:

- *Page does not say it*: Armstrong "could see every rock because each threw a long, sharp shadow" (ALSJ landing page: no); Apollo 11 landed "in early lunar morning" and "with the Sun behind him" (NASA SP-4029: no); "too low and shadows swallow the ground" (SP-4029 only describes the high-Sun washout); Athena's "panels facing the wrong way" and "deep cold" (SpacePolicyOnline: only Odysseus's panels pointed away, no mention of cold); "a day later Intuitive Machines said it did not expect Athena to recharge" (no page says it); "a small crater" (size not given); "the crater is too small for the map to show" (unsupported, replaced by the plain resolution fact); "Earth drifts a few degrees up and down" (no amplitude on SVS 10836); "Earth never in its sky" as a bare statement (replaced by the recorded Horizons rows); "your thumb at arm's length is about 2°" (the one NASA page found, NASA Ames, did not load; replaced by the little finger).
- *Could not open*: AMNH "Skylight: Apollo Mission Landings and Lunar Phases" (HTTP 403) was the planned source for "5° to 14° soon after local sunrise"; replaced by NASA SP-4029. Space.com's Queqiao article (only the headline loaded); Intuitive Machines' investor release (HTTP 403); NASA NSSDC Chang'e 4 record (error page); NASA Ames "thumb" page (connection refused).
